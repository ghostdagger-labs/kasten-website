FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Kasten free?
Yes. Kasten doesn’t charge for downloads or transactions. You only pay the Kaspa network fee, which goes to the network, not to us.
What happens if I lose my password?
There is no password reset, because we never have a copy. Reinstall Kasten or use another device, restore from your 12 or 24 words, and set a new password.
What if I lose my recovery phrase?
While you can still unlock Kasten, write down a new copy from Settings → Security → Show recovery phrase. If both the phrase and the device are lost, the funds cannot be recovered by anyone.
Can I move a wallet from another app?
Yes. Choose “I have a recovery phrase” and enter any standard 12- or 24-word Kaspa phrase.
Do the desktop app and the extension share a wallet?
Each one is complete on its own and keeps its own encrypted copy. To use the same wallet in both, restore it from your recovery phrase in each.
Can a website take my KAS?
No. A connected site can see your address and ask you to approve a transaction. Nothing is sent until you review it and type your password.
