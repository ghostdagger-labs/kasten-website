  • A public transaction ID only if needed and you are comfortable linking that blockchain activity to your email.

Common questions

I forgot my wallet password. What can I do?

There is no password-reset service. If you have your recovery phrase, restore it into a new wallet inside Kasten and choose a new password. Keep the existing encrypted wallet until you have checked that the restored addresses and accounts are correct.

If you have lost both the password and recovery phrase, GHOSTDAGGER cannot restore access. Do not send either secret to support or enter it on this website.

My balance or older transactions are missing.

Check that you selected the correct wallet, account, and network. Mainnet and Testnet-10 are separate networks, and Testnet funds are not Mainnet KAS. Allow the node connection and account scan to finish.

For older transactions, open Activity and choose “Discover older history.” Review its privacy disclosure before confirming: the explorer service will receive derived public addresses. If the balance still looks wrong, contact support before deleting or reinstalling anything.

Kasten is offline or cannot connect.

Check your internet connection. In Kasten, open Settings → Network and use “Retry connection.” A public resolver or node may be temporarily unavailable.

Close and reopen the popup or expanded view. If it continues, send the exact connection error, selected network, and approximate time to support. Reinstalling is not a first troubleshooting step because it removes local wallet data.

A transfer failed or its outcome is uncertain.

Check Activity and, if a transaction ID is available, inspect it using the matching network’s explorer. A lost connection does not necessarily mean a broadcast failed.

Avoid submitting the same payment again until you have checked its status and balance. If the result remains uncertain, contact support with the error and public transaction ID if available. Confirmed transfers cannot be reversed by GHOSTDAGGER.

Which recovery phrases and assets are supported?

The current Chrome extension supports native KAS on Mainnet and Testnet-10 and restoration of supported 12- or 24-word English recovery phrases. BIP39 passphrases, sometimes called an extra or “25th” word, are not supported in this version.

Kasten does not currently provide swaps, token or NFT support, or Ethereum compatibility. Wallet derivation and account discovery can differ between applications; check the restored addresses before relying on a restored wallet.

How do I disconnect a website?

Open Sites in Kasten and revoke the website’s connection, or disconnect all sites. Connecting allows a site to see your authorized public account information and request transfers; each transfer still requires a separate approval.

Disconnecting prevents future authorized access. It cannot undo a completed transfer or erase information the site already received.

Why does the wallet lock when I return?

Kasten locks after the configured browser-idle interval, when the operating system locks, or when its wallet runtime ends. Choose a five-, fifteen-, or sixty-minute idle interval in Settings → Auto-lock.

A browser restart or extension update can require unlocking again. Never send your wallet password to support to diagnose a locking issue.

What happens if I remove Kasten?

Uninstalling removes the extension’s local wallet storage. Kasten does not provide cloud backup or automatically synchronize your wallet with another installation.

Before uninstalling, securely back up your recovery phrase and verify that you can restore the correct wallet. Support cannot recover deleted local wallet data for you. Removing an extension does not remove public blockchain transactions.

Report a security concern.

Email support@ghostdagger.dev with the subject “Kasten security report.” Describe the affected version, what you observed, and reproduction steps using a disposable wallet if possible.

For a suspected vulnerability, avoid posting exploitation details publicly before we have had a chance to investigate. Never include real recovery phrases, private keys, passwords, or funds. If you suspect a connected website is malicious, reject its requests and revoke its connection in Sites.
